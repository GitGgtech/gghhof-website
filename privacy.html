OUR COMMITMENT

Privacy & safeguarding

Personal information

This starter website does not operate a visitor account system or store contact-form submissions. The contact form opens the visitor's email application. If the Foundation later adds online forms, mailing lists, analytics or donation processing, this page must be updated to explain what data is collected, why it is collected, who processes it and how people can exercise their rights.

Children and vulnerable people

We aim to protect the dignity, safety and privacy of everyone involved in our outreach. Obtain informed permission before publishing identifiable photographs, stories or videos. For children, obtain appropriate parent or guardian consent and avoid publishing full names, precise locations, school details or other information that could put them at risk.

Donations

Do not send card numbers, passwords or PINs by email or WhatsApp. Online donations should be processed only through a verified payment provider's secure checkout page. The Foundation should publish clear information about donation use, receipts and refund/contact procedures.
